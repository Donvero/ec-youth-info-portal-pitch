## 🎯 Executive Thesis
Youth unemployment in the Eastern Cape sits among the highest in South Africa. The primary barrier is not lack of ambition, but **asymmetric access to procurement opportunities, artisan apprenticeships, and statutory funding**.

The **Eastern Cape Youth Information Portal** acts as the digital clearinghouse connecting over 500,000 provincial youth directly to:
1. **Public Sector Learnerships & Bursaries** (SETA, TVET, and Provincial Treasury)
2. **SMME Micro-Procurement Work Packages** (CIDB Grades 1–2 Subcontracting)
3. **AI-Assisted CV & Compliance Document Generation** (SBD 4, Tax PIN, Sworn Affidavits)
